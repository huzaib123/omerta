THE CRAFT

I take raw footage and cut it into stories that hit hard. No over-engineered fluff — just clean pacing, intentional sound design, and color grading that sets the mood right away.

Years spent behind cinema rigs and inside editing bays. Whether it’s a fast-paced commercial, a music video, or a dark narrative short, I make sure every single frame earns its place.

Direct creative direction, zero friction, and an unreasonable sense of humor along the way.
